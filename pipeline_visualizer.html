🛡️ 7 Embedded Guardrails

1

Objective-Aware Prompts

Agent understands WHY it exists, not just WHAT to do. Guides decision-making in edge cases.

2

Steering Constraints

Soft boundaries in prompt layer guide agent thinking and trade-offs without hard enforcement.

3

Hard Constraints

Non-negotiable boundaries enforced in code. Cannot be bypassed by prompt manipulation.

4

Decision Boundaries

Clear separation: autonomous decisions vs escalation triggers. No ambiguity in authority.

5

Stop Rules

Complete/escalate/halt conditions prevent runaway behavior and infinite loops.

6

Health Metrics

Non-regression constraints prevent optimization shortcuts and quality degradation.

7

Escalation Triggers

Automatic detection of situations requiring human intervention. Pre-flight checking.
