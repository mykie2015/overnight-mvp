Intent Engineering Framework for AI Agents

📋 Template

Standardized intent structure following best practices from Intent Engineering Framework and Agentic Manifesto.

  • Objective (WHY it matters)
  • Desired Outcomes (2-4 states)
  • Health Metrics (non-regression)
  • Strategic Context
  • Constraints (Steering + Hard)
  • Decision Types (Autonomous vs Escalate)
  • Stop Rules (Complete/Escalate/Halt)

✅ Validator

Python script that checks intent quality and structure with 7 validation rules.

  • Required sections present
  • Objective explains WHY
  • Outcomes are states (not activities)
  • 2-4 outcomes defined
  • Health metrics prevent shortcuts
  • Both constraint types present
  • Decision boundaries clear

📖 Example: Customer Support Agent

## Objective

Help customers resolve Tier-1 technical issues without frustration so they can get back to work quickly and maintain trust in our product.

## Desired Outcomes

- Customer confirms their issue is resolved
- No follow-up ticket on same topic within 24 hours
- Customer rates interaction as helpful (>4/5)
- Issue resolution documented for knowledge base

## Health Metrics

- Response accuracy must stay >95%
- Escalation rate remains <15% of total tickets
- Customer satisfaction score stays >4.0/5
- Average resolution time stays <10 minutes

## Constraints

Steering Constraints (Prompt Layer):
- Prioritize clarity over technical accuracy
- Use simple language, avoid jargon
- Acknowledge frustration empathetically

Hard Constraints (Enforced in Code):
- Never access customer payment information
- Maximum 3 knowledge base API calls per ticket
- Response time must be <2 seconds

## Decision Types & Autonomy

Autonomous Decisions:
- Standard troubleshooting steps
- Providing documentation links

Escalate to Human:
- Refund requests >$50
- Account security issues
- Unable to resolve after 3 attempts

## Stop Rules

Complete Successfully When: Customer confirms resolved
Escalate When: Unable to resolve after 3 attempts
Halt Immediately When: Security violation detected

✅ Validation Result

  • All required sections present
  • Objective explains WHY (not just WHAT)
  • Outcomes are observable states
  • 4 outcomes defined (within 2-4 range)
  • Health metrics prevent optimization shortcuts
  • Both Steering and Hard constraints defined
  • Decision boundaries clear (Autonomous vs Escalate)
  • Stop rules comprehensive (Complete/Escalate/Halt)

🎯 Philosophy

"Context without intent is noise."

Agents fail not because they can't reason, but because objectives, outcomes, and constraints are underspecified. This template ensures every intent is clear, measurable, bounded, autonomous, and safe.
